# Mandecies vehicle academy assets

Nine original vehicle tutor portraits: `public/transport/guide-0.webp` through `guide-8.webp`. Generated from a 3×3 sheet with the prompt: premium cinematic 3D family animation, original friendly teal steam train, yellow excavator, blue propeller plane, coral cement mixer, green service truck, orange dump truck, purple passenger train, turquoise helicopter and golden cargo plane; sunlight, expressive faces, no lettering or franchise likenesses. Cropped to 480-pixel WebP portraits.

Nine original instrumental themes: `public/music/themes/vehicle-*.mp3`. Composed by `scripts/compose-themes.py` using layered mallet melodies, pads, bass, plucks and gentle percussion. No third-party samples.

Playable town geometry is procedural Three.js: moving train and plane, truck, rail line, road, crane, station buildings and golden learning markers. Quiet scenery freezes decorative movement; keyboard, touch buttons and helper ride support navigation.

Kindergarten starter curriculum: 27 original lessons across nine subjects. Core standard references checked against NC DPI's Kindergarten Quick Reference Guide: https://www.dpi.nc.gov/documents/publications/catalog/is183-quick-reference-guide-k/open . Enrichment subjects are labeled as such. This is not a claim of a complete school-year curriculum.

Childlike speech is an original youthful character direction with clear phonics articulation. Natural speech needs an existing OpenAI or ElevenLabs key and service credits. Voice previews allow caregivers to choose each tutor; no live paid-service audio was evaluated during development.
