# Madeline’s Celestial Academy artwork

Original AI-generated artwork created with the imagegen skill and builtin image-generation tool; no existing franchise characters or real people were requested. The six guides are fictional male young adults, represented as friendly academic mentors.

## Shipped assets

- `public/madeline/guide-0.webp`: Kaito, East Asian reading guide, navy and teal.
- `public/madeline/guide-1.webp`: Ren, Black mathematics guide, emerald and cream.
- `public/madeline/guide-2.webp`: Sora, East Asian science guide, plum and silver.
- `public/madeline/guide-3.webp`: Akira, Black writing guide, burgundy and gold.
- `public/madeline/guide-4.webp`: Haru, East Asian social-studies guide, copper and moss.
- `public/madeline/guide-5.webp`: Noah, Black music and focus guide, indigo and gold.
- `public/madeline/academy.webp`: original floating celestial academy at warm dusk.

## Generation and edit prompts

Portrait generation: six original handsome, fully clothed academy guides, three East Asian and three Black, arranged in an exact three-column/two-row sheet with no lettering or gutters. Friendly classroom expressions, individualized hair and academy jackets, natural proportions, rich warm celestial backgrounds. No known people, franchise characters, flirting or romance.

Portrait refinement (builtin edit mode, using the original sheet): preserve each identity, ethnic appearance, costume and grid position. Make the guides look like original live-action actors aged 18–22, with actual human face anatomy, realistic iris sizes, natural skin texture and pores, realistic hair and clear front-facing expressions. Keep mouths, eyes, cheeks and chin unobstructed, hands away from faces, and neutral gentle smiles suitable for D-ID. Retain the anime influence in costumes and magical academy scenery.

Academy generation (builtin generation mode): cinematic warm-dusk floating-island celestial academy, Japanese-inspired rooftops and a futuristic observatory, glass sky bridges, giant planet and soft aurora above a cloud sea, terrace gardens, teal water, copper and ivory architecture. Original fantasy environment, no text, known characters or logos.

The refined 1536×1024 portrait sheet was cropped into six 512×512 WebP portraits. The academy artwork was converted to WebP. D-ID uploads convert the bundled WebP portrait into a real PNG in the renderer before upload.

## Talking video preparation

These are still portraits, not already-created D-ID videos. The installed app’s Talking Tutor Studio can prepare seven starter clips per tutor, or all fixed lesson lines, using a caregiver-supplied paid D-ID API key. A face/voice-specific namespace separates new recordings from older clips. Successful MP4 clips are saved on disk; repeated preparation skips existing clips. Dynamic homework and isolated phonics use live speech.

Browser tests use a clearly synthetic MP4 fixture and mocked D-ID responses to exercise upload, cache, playback, music ducking and authentication errors. No production D-ID account or paid voice service was used to validate perceptual voice quality. Caregivers should preview and choose their preferred voices.

The 33 scaffolded lessons are a starter bank, not a complete school-year curriculum. Academic targets reference NC DPI’s Grade 6 Quick Reference Guide (2025): https://www.dpi.nc.gov/documents/publications/catalog/is189-quick-reference-guide-6th/open . Music/focus activities are enrichment.
